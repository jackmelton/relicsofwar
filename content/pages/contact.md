## Before you write

- **About a listing** (price, condition, availability, shipping): contact the seller — every listing links to the dealer or auction house that offers it. Relics of War does not sell anything.
- **To value or authenticate your piece:** we do not appraise or authenticate individual items. Our [identification guides](/identify/) and [price guide](/price-guide/) will help you ask the right questions of a qualified specialist.
- **To report an error:** see the [Corrections Policy](/corrections/).
- **Dealers and auction houses** who want their inventory in the marketplace: start at [ArtifactSearch.com](https://artifactsearch.com/).
- **Membership** (free): [create an account on ArtifactSearch](https://artifactsearch.com/account/register).
