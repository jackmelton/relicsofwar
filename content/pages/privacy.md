Relics of War (relicsofwar.com) is published by Historical Publications LLC, Blue Ridge, Georgia.

## What we collect

Relics of War is a static website. It has no accounts, no login, no forms, and no shopping cart. We do not set tracking cookies and we do not sell or share personal information.

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) records standard, aggregated web-server information — such as pages requested, approximate region, browser type, and referring site — to operate and secure the service. We may view that traffic in aggregate; it does not identify you to us.

## Links to other sites

Listings, photographs, and price records link to ArtifactSearch.com and to dealers and auction houses. Those sites have their own privacy practices. Membership, saved searches, and alerts are provided by ArtifactSearch.com under its own [privacy policy](https://artifactsearch.com/privacy).

Photographs of listings are loaded through ArtifactSearch's image service so that pages load quickly; that request carries the same standard server information described above.

## Email

If you email us we keep the correspondence to answer you. We do not add you to any mailing list without asking.

## Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and note the review date above.
