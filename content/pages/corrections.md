We correct errors promptly and visibly. If you find a factual mistake in an identification guide, a mislabeled listing, a price-guide figure that does not match its underlying records, or a broken page, tell us:

- Email **info@historicalpublicationsllc.com** with the page address and what is wrong.
- Or call **800-777-1862**.

## What happens next

- **Guides and editorial text:** we check the claim against the references, correct the page, and note the correction on it.
- **Listing data:** listing text and photographs are the seller's; we pass the report to ArtifactSearch, which contacts the seller or removes the listing.
- **Price-guide figures:** figures are computed automatically from recorded sales. If an underlying sale record is wrong it is corrected at the source and the guide updates on the next nightly rebuild.

We do not remove accurate historical information because it is unwelcome; we do remove anything we cannot stand behind.
