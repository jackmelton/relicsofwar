Our Purpose

History You Can Hold

Relics of War is a curated home for authentic military artifacts — from Civil War accoutrements and edged weapons to insignia, photographs, and the everyday objects carried by the men who served. Each piece is researched, documented, and presented with the accuracy and respect its history deserves.

Built by collectors and historians, for collectors and historians.
